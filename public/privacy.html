MATON Kaye privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

MATON Kaye is a free math tutor for students in Haiti, built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026. A student sends a photo of handwritten math homework, through the web app at maton-adaptive-tutor.pages.dev or through WhatsApp, and receives an explanation in Haitian Creole and practice exercises.

What we receive

  • The notebook photo you send.
  • The answers you type to practice exercises.
  • On WhatsApp: your phone number and message IDs, as delivered by WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • The photo is sent to Meta's Model API (Muse Spark and SAM 3.1) to read the work and write the explanation. Meta processes it under its own terms.
  • Our code checks the math and creates practice exercises.
  • On WhatsApp, we use your phone number only to reply to you.

What we keep

  • We do not store photos. They are discarded once the answer is sent.
  • On WhatsApp, we keep your current practice exercise for 1 hour, under a scrambled (hashed) form of your phone number, never the number itself. Message IDs are kept the same way for 24 hours so the same message is not answered twice. Both are deleted automatically.
  • We do not write phone numbers or message text to our logs.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use the data for anything else.

Who hosts it

The app runs on Cloudflare Pages. WhatsApp messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Students

MATON Kaye is meant for students aged 10 to 14 with the agreement of a parent, guardian or teacher. Please do not put names, faces or other personal details in the photos.

Your choices

You can stop at any time by no longer sending messages. Because we keep nothing beyond the limits above, there is normally nothing to delete. To ask a question or request deletion, write to jules001cesar@gmail.com.

An kreyòl

MATON Kaye se yon tutè matematik gratis pou elèv ann Ayiti. Ou voye foto kaye ou, sou sit la oswa sou WhatsApp, epi ou resevwa yon eksplikasyon an kreyòl ak egzèsis pou pratike.

  • Foto a ale kay Meta (Muse Spark ak SAM 3.1) pou li kaye a. Nou pa kenbe foto yo.
  • Sou WhatsApp, nou sèvi ak nimewo w sèlman pou n reponn ou. Nou kenbe egzèsis ou ap fè a pandan 1 èdtan, anba yon fòm nimewo a ki melanje (hash), pa nimewo a menm. Apre sa li efase poukont li.
  • Nou pa vann done, nou pa mete piblisite.
  • Tanpri pa mete non, figi oswa lòt enfòmasyon pèsonèl nan foto yo.
